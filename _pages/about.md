I am an Assistant Professor ("Professor Adjunto") at the Engineering School of the Polytechnic Institute of Lisbon ([ISEL](https://www.isel.pt) // [IPL](https://www.ipl.pt)), where I work in distributed systems, cloud computing, and computer security. I hold a PhD in Informatics and Computer Engineering from ULisboa (IST), supervised by Prof. [Luís Veiga](https://www.dpss.inesc-id.pt/~lveiga/), and I am a researcher at INESC-ID Lisboa in the [Distributed, Parallel and Secure Systems](https://www.inesc-id.pt/research-areas/distributed-parallel-and-secure-systems/). At ISEL, I am a member of [Future Internet Technologies (FIT)](https://fit.isel.pt), a research group with strong ties to society.

My research focuses on cloud infrastructure and services, particularly serverless architectures, as well as computer security, with results published in several high-impact journals and international conferences. I have also taken part in consultancy projects with government departments, municipalities, and private organizations. I currently serve on the coordination board of the Master's in Computer Engineering and Informatics ([MEIC](https://www.isel.pt/en/curso/masters-degree/master-computer-science-and-engineering)) and am a member of the Technical-Scientific Council at ISEL.
